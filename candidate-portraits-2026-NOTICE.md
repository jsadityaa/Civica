# Candidate portraits

`candidate-portraits-2026.js` identifies portraits by the candidate IDs in the 2026 results feed. Wikimedia imports are matched by name and election state and checked against Commons metadata for public-domain, CC0, or Creative Commons Attribution/Attribution-ShareAlike licensing. Additional photographs come from public campaign, government, nonprofit, and candidate profile pages; those photographs retain their source copyright and are not represented as Creative Commons images. Each record retains its source and available attribution. Portraits link to their source and show attribution in the link title. Local copies are in `assets/candidates/2026/`.

`import-candidate-portraits-2026.py` imports biography portraits; `import-campaign-portraits-2026.py` supplements them using public voter guides matched by state, name, and party. The unresolved list tracks candidates without a verified usable individual portrait. Unknown portraits must not be filled with another person's image, campaign logos, or unidentified group photographs. Grouped vote totals do not represent a single candidate and never receive a person's portrait. Existing headshots from the site's earlier candidate image mappings remain fallbacks; election results always use the 2026 data.

`expand-candidates-2026.py` builds `race-candidates-2026-data.js` from county-return identities and public state ballot guides. Named candidates replace grouped rows in candidate tables. Individual totals are supplied only when county sums reconcile exactly with the saved national snapshot. Otherwise their vote and percentage cells remain unavailable, with the grouped vote total disclosed separately. A snapshot timestamp prevents old supplemental counts being shown after the results snapshot changes. Portrait imports also include these additional candidate IDs.
