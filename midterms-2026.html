2026 Midterm Summary

The 2026 United States midterm elections are scheduled for Tuesday, November 3, 2026. The cycle will serve as the first national test of Donald Trump's second administration, with control of Congress, the direction of federal policy, and the strength of both party coalitions on the line.

Contents of the Ballot

All 435 seats in the House of Representatives are up for election. In the Senate, 35 seats will be contested: 33 regularly scheduled races and special elections in Florida and Ohio. Governorships, state legislative seats, and local offices will also appear on many state and local ballots.

POLYCIVIC FORECAST RELEASE

Polycivic's 2026 midterm forecasts are scheduled to release on Tuesday, October 27, 2026, one week before Election Day. The first forecast package will focus on the House and Senate, with additional coverage areas added later if the model expands.

Voter Resources

Voting rules, registration deadlines, early voting options, and absentee ballot procedures vary by state. Voters should check their state election office or local election board for official, location-specific guidance as the election approaches.
