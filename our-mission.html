Our Vision

Our vision is to make electoral information easier to follow, trust, and understand. Polycivic is designed to be a reliable starting point for understanding consequential elections across the United States.

We give users one place to track election movement, explore the factors shaping our pre-election analysis, and compare those expectations with the final results. By bringing forecasts, underlying indicators, and post-election analysis together, Polycivic aims to provide clear context for how an election developed and why it mattered.
